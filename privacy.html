Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The figures you type into the budget planner, and your location if you choose to show it on the map, stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

What you type stays on your device

The savings, income, rent and other figures you enter in the budget planner are saved in your own browser (its "local storage") so they're there when you come back. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Your browser also remembers small settings, such as whether you've seen the guided tour. You can clear all of this at any time in your browser's settings.

Your location

If you tap the location button on the map, your browser asks your permission and then shows your position on the map, following you until you tap the button again. Your location is used only inside your browser to draw the dot. It is never sent to us, never stored by the site, and forgotten when you switch it off or leave the page. Your browser may remember whether you allowed location for this site; you can change that in your browser's site settings.

When the map moves to your position, it loads map pictures for that area from the map providers listed below, as it does whenever you move the map. Those requests show the provider roughly which area you are viewing, along with your IP address, as with any online map.

What we collect

Nothing about you. Plotted has no sign-up, sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. If we add privacy-friendly visitor counting in future, it will not use cookies or identify you, and we'll update this page first.

Services the page relies on

Like most websites, the page loads files from other providers. When your browser requests them, those providers receive your IP address and basic browser details as part of the normal web request, and handle them under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site
  • Google Fonts, for the typefaces
  • unpkg, for the map software
  • OpenFreeMap, for the street map
  • NSW Spatial Services, for the aerial imagery

The property records

The site shows addresses, sale prices, sale dates and land details from public NSW Government records. It shows no names of buyers or sellers. If you think a record is wrong, or you have a safety reason for asking us to hide an address, email us and we'll look at it promptly.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don't add you to any list.
