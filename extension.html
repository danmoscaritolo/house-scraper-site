plotted

Check listings as you browse

Plotted for Chrome, Edge and Brave · version 0.5 · free

Open any NSW listing on domain.com.au or realestate.com.au and a small Plotted card shows how its price compares with what similar homes nearby sold for, what sold in the same building or street, the home's last sale, the suburb's trend and whether it fits your budget.

The card answers three things:
  • Is the price in line? Below, in line with or above recent sales (or rents) of similar homes nearby, moved to today's market.
  • What sold right here? Every recent sale in the same apartment building, or on the same street for houses.
  • Can I afford it? Within, a stretch or over the limit you set in Your budget on Plotted, worked out on your device.

Install (2 minutes)

  1. Download the extension above and unzip it. You get a folder called plotted-extension.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions (in Edge, edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open a NSW listing on Domain or realestate.com.au. The card appears in the bottom-right corner.
  5. Optional: set up Your budget on Plotted once, in the same browser, so cards can say whether a price fits.

To update, download the new version, replace the folder and press the reload button on the extension in chrome://extensions.

How it works

The extension reads the facts shown on the listing you have open: address, property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, car spaces, size and the price as displayed. It then compares them with NSW Valuer General sales and NSW Fair Trading rental bonds, using the same public data as this website. Sales records don't include bedrooms, so apartments are compared with others in the same building first, then nearby.

It's a comparison with recorded sales, not a valuation or advice, and it can't see a home's condition, views or renovations.

Privacy

No account and no tracking. The extension doesn't record which listings you look at or collect listing data from your browsing, and your budget limits stay on your device. Details are in the Privacy policy.

Phones

Phone browsers don't run extensions. On a phone, search the address on the Plotted map to see every recorded sale of the home.
